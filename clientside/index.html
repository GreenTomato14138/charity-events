Who we are

Some background information about the organisation, shown on the home page.

Our mission

To make giving easy, transparent and genuinely fun. We connect neighbours, schools and local businesses with the charities doing the unglamorous, essential work — and we publish exactly where every dollar ends up.

How it works

Charities publish an event, the public browses and registers, and 100% of the ticket price goes to the cause. Corporate partners match community donations dollar for dollar at the end of each month.

Our promise

Every event is reviewed against our fundraising policy before it goes live. Anything that breaches the policy is suspended immediately and removed from all public pages.
